Driven by Technology.
Built on Corporate Trust.

Allied-Iconic Pvt. Ltd., operating with its technology and engineering division Allied Infotech, was established in the year 2021 by a board of experienced technocrats. We represent leading global manufacturing principals, supplying high-performance moulding systems, descaling solutions, and tooling components to factories across South India.

Our leadership combines three decades of institutional marketing, technical service support, and industrial sales expertise. We bridge international trade partnerships with local service desks in Chennai to support insert moulding and toggle injection systems.

Vision

To deliver high-precision engineering accessories that reduce cycle times and support manufacturing excellence in India.

Mission

To configure safe, wear-resistant clamping platens, screw barrels, and eco-safe descaling systems with Chennai service support.
