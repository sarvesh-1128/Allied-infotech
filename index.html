About Allied Infotech

Allied Infotech is founded by engineering professionals with deep experience in sales, marketing, mobility, oil and lubricants, industrial consumables, and injection moulding technologies.

Company Overview

Established in 2017, Allied Infotech represents leading global manufacturers of injection moulding machines and industrial components in India.

We operate with a skilled workforce and service-oriented approach to ensure seamless support for customers in production, maintenance, and moulding technology adoption.

Our market reach spans major cities across South India, with the ability to support nationwide business development.
